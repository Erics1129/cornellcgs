# 保卫萝卜: mechanics reference for an original 3D fan game

Research date: 12 October 2026. This is reference research, not a specification claiming to reproduce a particular commercial version. User direction: recognizable carrot-defense fan project; original artwork and maps; cinematic storybook materials; fixed-path tower defense for phone and desktop.

## Towers, enemies and maps: specific verified interactions

The official 保卫萝卜4 feature page documents air/ground missiles, bubble slow, underwater torpedoes, chain lightning, controllable conveyors, lamps recharged by attacks, reward chests, a coin-producing target, and an enemy that encases towers in jelly until it is removed. These are **theme-specific examples**, not mechanics present in every level. The strategic pattern is a clear threat with a visible counter, with scenery sometimes acting as a resource or tactical device. [Official 保卫萝卜4 feature page](https://luobo4.737.com/m/)

An official 2022 slowdown guide distinguishes single-target bubbles, an area snowball freeze, bouncing moon projectiles that affect airborne and ground enemies, and a faster-firing single-target slow tower. Thus, two towers with the same headline effect should still differ in coverage and targeting. [Official slowdown guide](https://www.taptap.cn/moment/311939306774072042?group_id=308328)

The official prelaunch enemy guide distinguishes ordinary low-health enemies, progressively shielded enemies, and fliers that travel directly toward the carrot rather than following the ground route; only appropriate air-defense towers can hit those fliers. The same announcement, mirrored by the game's 阿波本波 account on Xiaomi, describes fast low-health skaters and a giant that accelerates after losing its shield and takes two carrot health on arrival. These are 2022-era examples, not a full current enemy list. [Official test-forum enemy guide](https://www.taptap.cn/moment/283675713788184911?group_id=327475), [same announcement on Xiaomi](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1385871792_1654687667713_13)

A 2024 official magic-theme introduction gives a concrete relationship between route geometry and weapons: its spinning-top projectile splits and rolls both directions along the road, so junction placement improves coverage. A speed-buff tile and a rescued owl introduce other choices. [Official magic-theme introduction](https://www.taptap.cn/moment/531498373878908561)

The same theme's official finale describes a three-headed boss whose heads respectively freeze towers, reduce their attack speed and occupy/destroy tower spaces. Players can focus an individual head to stun it, making the remaining heads more aggressive. This supports a boss built around readable phases and target choice instead of only an enormous health bar. [Official boss finale](https://www.taptap.cn/moment/549596622682587298)

## Proposed original systems — design decisions, not copied game data

1. **Core loop:** choose a campaign map → place initial towers → start a wave → earn coins from defeated monsters and cleared obstacles → upgrade, sell or build → survive the final wave → earn a carrot medal and advance. Enemy routes are authored and visible. Tapping a monster/obstacle sets a focus target; tapping again clears focus. Towers resume their ordinary target policy automatically when that target disappears.
2. **Win and loss:** win only when every scheduled spawn has occurred and every living enemy has either been defeated or resolved its carrot attack. Lose when carrot health reaches zero. Freeze gameplay behind the outcome sheet. Offer a prominent next-level action after victory and immediate retry after defeat; no energy or retry toll.
3. **Carrot health and rewards:** use ten leaf/heart units as an original tuning choice, not a verified universal series constant. Most enemies bite once; clearly large elites take two units. Award survival, untouched carrot, and scenery-clear achievements separately. Show progress with readable icons and a wave counter, not paragraph instructions.
4. **Carrot-hit choreography:** approaching enemy commits to a brief, interruptible-before-contact bite windup; the actual contact resolves once. Carrot recoils, squashes, squeezes its eyes, sheds a few harmless orange/leaf particles, then recovers with a short wobble. An audible crunch and the lost health pip coincide with contact. The offender exits or dissolves after one attack. Prevent repeated damage during one animation, and do not hide the board with a cinematic camera cut.
5. **Starting tower roster:** rapid single-target seed cannon; splash mortar; frost blossom with modest damage and strong slow; long-range piercing thorn; chain-lightning storm bell; expensive sunburst area tower. Three readable upgrade stages per tower, increasing silhouette and animation strength as well as stats. Slow effects use the strongest active slow rather than multiplying into a permanent freeze. Bosses have bounded control resistance.
6. **Enemy variety:** at least eight distinct bodies and locomotion styles: soft seedling hopper, fast leaf-mouse runner, low armored acorn beetle, tall marsh brute, airborne moth, splitting spore creature, healing lantern snail, and a multi-phase root guardian. Introduce one behavior before combining it with others. Do not make the roster only recolored rabbits. Each needs anticipation, locomotion, attack/contact, hit reaction and defeat animation references before production.
7. **Wave pacing:** begin with short teaching levels of 5–7 waves, grow to 8–12 in the main campaign, and use longer finales only if playtesting supports them. A visible wave-preview strip shows icons of upcoming roles. Allow a preparation pause and optional early send; later waves alternate swarms, runners and armor rather than only scaling health. These numbers are new-game targets, not facts about 保卫萝卜4.
8. **Economy and targeting:** clearing an obstacle yields coins and/or opens a useful tower pad, creating a choice between expansion and immediate defense. Towers normally prioritize greatest route progress, with an explicit focused target taking precedence. Air eligibility is visible before purchase. Refund part of invested coins on sale; show the exact refund before the action. Preserve tower upgrades and save progress reliably between visits.
9. **Progression:** three original biomes with four authored maps each is a useful initial production scope: Garden Dawn, Lantern Marsh, and Frostglass Orchard. Each chapter introduces a tower and enemy counter, recombines it, then ends with a boss. Retain the original series' repeatable mastery rather than adding an energy economy, ads, purchases or a large metagame.
10. **Effects and sound:** environmental lighting may be realistic while silhouettes remain playful. Keep attack impacts short and concentrated at contact; reserve the longest effects for bosses, tower upgrades and victories. Give each family a recognizable sound timbre, use multiple hit variations, and reduce simultaneous voices during swarms. Music should gain percussion/intensity for later waves and release into a warm victory cue. No product UI should mention generation tools or show production-status bars.

## Uncertainties and boundaries

- No first-party numerical combat table or current complete balance sheet was found. Do not claim exact enemy HP, tower DPS, spawn intervals, upgrade costs, refund ratios or universal carrot-health rules were reverse-engineered. Tune and test the new game independently.
- The original site's 15–25-wave statement is version-specific marketing copy. Current 保卫萝卜4 has many themes and special modes; wave lengths and rules can differ.
- “金萝卜”, scenery-clear recognition and thematic progression are verified reference concepts. The proposed three-achievement implementation and exact scoring conditions are project design, not a statement that all series entries use that system.
- Official references show many rabbit variants in 4; the user's request for significantly different monsters justifies a broader original silhouette roster. Preserve the carrot-defense premise without copying promotional artwork, individual maps, animations, audio, or exact commercial UI.
- Cinematic quality is an art/performance goal. Generated reference sheets alone are not finished 3D animation; every promised motion must be implemented and inspected in the actual Unity build on phone-sized and desktop views.
