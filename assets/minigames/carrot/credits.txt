# Carrot Defense asset credits and provenance

## Original work

Carrot model with articulated eyes, brows, limbs, leaves and mouth states; ten tower families with four physically distinct tiers; custom boss bodies/armor/crowns/antlers/tentacles; and creature costume additions were authored in Blender for this project. Model authoring sources are in `Tools/build_carrot_models.py`, `Tools/sculpt_carrot_details.py` and `Tools/build_carrot_characters.py`. Twenty-two generated reference plates (the fourth chapter's `23-orchard-keyart.jpg` is now a generated painting and `25-hollowhead-menacing.jpg` a crop of the generated `34-hollowhead-sheet.jpg` turnaround), a material atlas, an effects atlas and four generated biological albedos (carrot skin, leaf tissue, white fur and teal cephalopod skin) were used for art direction and surface detail. Generated artwork is separate from the licensed CC0 source assets below. The biological color images are supplemented by eight original analytic normal/roughness textures authored in `Tools/bake_biological_relief.py` (carrot creases/pores, leaf veins, fur fibers and soft pebbled hide). They are authored approximations, not measured PBR scans. Exact texture hashes and technical ranges are in `Assets/Resources/Carrot/Models/Materials/biological-relief-provenance.json`. These are genuinely modeled meshes, not image planes.

## Generated 2D plates for the Super tier and the boss challenges

Eight further generated images were made for the website page. They are flat 2D plates, sprite sheets and portraits used only by the HTML interface (level cards, the tower aside, the collection and the boss bar); none is a 3D asset, texture or model, and the in-game Super towers and challenge bosses are separate Blender geometry. `26-super-forms.jpg` is a 5x2 sheet of the ten Super tower portraits (seed, frost, mortar, prism, storm / blossom, starfruit, gravity, book, thunder); `27-super-emblems.png` is a 5x2 sheet of round golden sigils on transparency in the same order (re-encoded at 768x512 to stay under the page's image budget); `28-tornado-keyart.jpg`, `29-zombie-keyart.jpg` and `30-eyeball-keyart.jpg` are the challenge card paintings; `31-arena-keyart.jpg` is the arena loading plate; `32-challenge-bosses-menacing.jpg` stacks the three boss character sheets (tornado, zombie, eyeball; the first pose of each row is the HUD portrait); `33-ability-glyphs.png` is a 3x2 sheet of round ability icons (blind, uproot, thornwall / tithe, whiteout, crowcurse). `35-tornado-sheet.jpg`, `36-zombie-sheet.jpg` and `37-eyeball-sheet.jpg` are generated turnaround sheets used as modelling references. Generated artwork is separate from the licensed CC0 source assets below.

## Scope and rendering limits

The runtime uses optimized real-time geometry, skeletal animation, authored effects and generated texture details. It is not an offline feature-film renderer. Every tower tier has different geometry. The animated model inventory is in `Tools/character-model-manifest.json`; the original tower/carrot inventory is in `Tools/original-model-manifest.json`. Root integration must validate Unity/WebGL performance and gameplay timing on actual browser hardware.

## Portal expansion: additional original models, portraits and audio

The original game remains intact. The expansion adds ten tower families, each with four paid upgrade forms and a merged Super form; fifteen distinct minibosses, twelve ordinary creature types and the Portal boss. New native rigs and mesh authoring sources are in Tools/build_portal_towers.py and Tools/build_portal_creatures.py, with inventory manifests. Generated interface plates 38 and40–44 contain the new tower references, Portal painting,15miniboss portraits,12creature portraits and50upgrade portraits. These are separate from the actual animated geometry. A four-region generated biological albedo atlas provides chitin, mushroom, bamboo and fruit-skin color detail. They are original generated/authored assets; generated albedo is not a measured scan.
