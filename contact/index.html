How our AI learns

Self-play

  1. 01Frame. Turn the rules of the game into code.
  2. 02Simulate. Build a fast table where bots can play each other.
  3. 03Train. Let them play game after game and learn from every win and loss.
  4. 04Evaluate. Put the new bot against the old one to see if it got better.
  5. 05Ship. Share it once it holds up.

  • 3D · Push your luck

    Enter the forge →

  • 25 levels

    Ink Maze

    Roll a ball to color every floor block before the ink runs out. Boards grow from 4 × 4 on level 1 to 9 × 9, and the last board, level 25, is a small 8 × 8 that takes the most planning. Each level gives you a set amount of ink, and on 12 of them there is none to spare.

    Play Ink Maze →

Ink Maze

Roll the ball to color every floor block before you run out of ink.

  1. Pick a direction and the ball rolls in a straight line until a wall or the edge of the board stops it.
  2. Every block the ball rolls onto costs one ink, even if it is already colored.
  3. If the ink runs out partway through a roll, the ball stops on the block where it ran out.
  4. Color every floor block to clear the level, even if that takes your last drop of ink.
  5. If the ink runs out while some blocks are still uncolored, you lose the level. You can still undo or restart.

Page/privacy/

Privacy

What the site keeps when you sign in to a game, and how to remove it.

  • Signed out

    Default

    Every game works without an account. Progress stays in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.

  • Signed in

    With Google

    Signing in with Google adds a server copy of that same progress so it follows you to other devices. We store a hash of your Google account id, your display name, your profile picture link, and your game saves. No email address, no password, no token from Google.

  • Signing out

    All devices

    Sign out on any device and every other device is signed out too. Handy on a shared computer.

  • Deleting

    Your call

    Open the sign-in menu in any game and choose Delete account. Your server data is removed at once. What is in your browser stays, and you keep playing signed out.

  • Google

    Their side

    Google records that you used Sign in with Google here. You can review or remove that in your Google account under connections.

    Google account connections

  • Questions

    Contact

    Write to the club and we will answer or delete on request.

Page/terms/

Terms

The short version of using this site and its games.

  • Free to use

    No cost

    The site and the games are free. No purchases, no ads, no paid tiers.

  • Accounts

    Optional

    An account only carries game progress between devices. Keep your Google account secure; we cannot recover a sign-in for you.

  • Fair play

    Scores

    Leaderboards are for play by hand. Scripted or forged scores may be removed without notice.

  • Availability

    Best effort

    A student club runs this site. Games and sign-in can be down or change at any time, and saved progress can be lost. Nothing here is a warranty.

  • Content

    Ownership

    Game code and artwork belong to the club or their listed authors. Third-party assets are used under their own licenses.

  • Changes

    This page

    We may update these terms. The date of the last change is in the site repository history.
